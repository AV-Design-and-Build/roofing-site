# Proof Page Template

Use this template only when real project details are available.

## Required Inputs

- City and neighborhood, if approved.
- Service type.
- Roof problem.
- Approved photos.
- What was inspected.
- What work was completed.
- Whether the project involved repair, replacement, storm damage, emergency response, financing, or insurance-help conversations.
- Owner-approved privacy level.

## Page Structure

1. Hero
   - City.
   - Service.
   - Real project photo.
   - Phone CTA.

2. Problem
   - What the property owner noticed.
   - Why it mattered.
   - What was inspected.

3. Work Completed
   - Scope summary.
   - Materials or details only when verified.
   - Before/after images if available.

4. Trust And Next Step
   - No invented customer quote.
   - Link to relevant service page.
   - Link to `/estimate`.
   - Phone CTA with `data-track-event="phone_click"`.

## Guardrails

- Do not publish a fake customer story.
- Do not invent reviewer names.
- Do not turn stock imagery into claimed project proof.
- Do not claim a city office where none exists.
